Doing it in PowerPoint instead

If you'd rather stay in PowerPoint, here are the three ways it can be done there. Each works in PowerPoint for Windows and Mac.

Delete the animations on one slide

  1. Go to the slide and open the Animations tab.
  2. Click Animation Pane. A list of the slide's animations opens on the right.
  3. Click inside that list and press Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on a Mac) to select them all.
  4. Press Delete.

PowerPoint has no button for all slides at once, so you repeat this on every slide. For a long deck, the tool above is quicker.

Switch animations off without deleting them

  1. Open the Slide Show tab and click Set Up Slide Show.
  2. Tick Show without animation and click OK.

The animations stay in the file but won't play when you present. Untick the box to bring them back. This only affects presenting: if you send the file to someone, their copy still animates.

Remove transitions from every slide

  1. Open the Transitions tab and click None.
  2. Click Apply To All.
Delete themBest before printing, saving as PDF, sharing, or starting the animations again from scratch.
Switch them offBest if you only need a quick run-through and want the animations back later.

Questions

How do I remove all animations from PowerPoint at once?

Upload your deck above and press Remove animations. Every animation on every slide is taken out in one go, and you download a clean copy. PowerPoint itself has no single button for this; there you have to clear each slide separately.

How do I remove animations in PowerPoint without a tool?

On each slide: open the Animations tab, click Animation Pane, click inside the pane and press Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on a Mac) to select every animation, then press Delete. Repeat for each slide.

Can I switch animations off without deleting them?

Yes. In PowerPoint go to Slide Show, then Set Up Slide Show, and tick Show without animation. The animations stay in the file but don't play when you present. Untick it to bring them back.

Does it remove transitions too?

Only if you want. Turn on Also remove slide transitions and the slide-to-slide effects go as well. In PowerPoint you can do this yourself with Transitions, then None, then Apply To All.

Can I remove animations from only some slides?

Yes. In Which slides, type the slides you want cleared, for example 2-10, 14. Leave it as all for the whole deck.

Will anything else on my slides change?

No. Text, pictures, layouts, notes and the order of slides stay exactly as they were. Only the animation instructions are taken out.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. The tool runs in your browser and your file stays on your device.

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no watermark. You can clean several decks at once.
