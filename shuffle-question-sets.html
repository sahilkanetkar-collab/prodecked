How it works

  3. Download one zipPDF papers, slide decks and an answer-key PDF and CSV for every set.

Questions

Will “All of the above” end up in the wrong place?

No. Questions whose options refer to other options (all/none of the above, both A and B) keep their option order.

Can I get the same sets again?

Yes. The shuffle is based on your questions, so the same paper always produces the same sets.

How do I match a set's question to the original?

The answer-key PDF and CSV show each question's original number in brackets.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs in your browser and your file stays on your device.

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no watermark on your output.
