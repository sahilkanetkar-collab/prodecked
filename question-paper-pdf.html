Questions

Can it print Hindi or Gujarati?

Not yet. PDFs use a standard print font that covers English and European languages. Use the quiz deck for other scripts for now.

Can I add my institute's name?

Put it in the title, for example: Weekly test 7 · Your Institute · 30 minutes.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs in your browser and your file stays on your device.

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no watermark on your output.
