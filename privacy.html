Privacy

Your files never leave your device

Every ProDecked tool runs inside your web browser. When you add a PowerPoint file, the page reads it on your computer or phone, makes the change there, and gives the new file back to you as a download. The file is not sent to ProDecked or to anyone else.

You can check this yourself: open a tool, switch off your internet connection, and use it. It still works, because nothing needs to be sent anywhere.

What we do collect

Like most websites, we count page visits (which pages were opened, roughly where from, on what kind of device) using privacy-friendly analytics without cookies. We never see the names or contents of your files.

Your tool settings (for example, the watermark text you last used) are remembered in your own browser so you don't have to type them again. You can clear them by clearing this site's data in your browser.
