How it works

  1. Paste your questionsNumbered questions with A/B/C/D options and an Answer line. Excel or CSV works too.
  2. Pick a lookFive classroom-ready themes, with an optional title slide and timer.
  3. Download the .pptxOpen it in PowerPoint or Google Slides and press F5.

Questions

Can I edit the deck afterwards?

Yes. Every question, option and colour is ordinary PowerPoint text and shapes, so you can change anything.

Does it work with Google Slides?

The deck opens in Google Slides. Click-by-click reveals work there too; the countdown bar needs PowerPoint.

How many questions can I add?

As many as you like. A 100-question deck builds in a few seconds on a normal laptop.

What if a question has no answer?

It still gets a slide. The options reveal one by one but no tick is shown, and the checker above the button tells you which questions are missing answers.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs in your browser and your file stays on your device.

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no watermark on your output.
