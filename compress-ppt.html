Questions

Will my slides look blurry?

Balanced keeps images up to 1920 pixels wide, which is full-HD projector resolution.

Why is my deck still big?

Videos and audio aren't compressed. The report lists what's taking the space.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs in your browser and your file stays on your device.

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no watermark on your output.
