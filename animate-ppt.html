How it works

  1. Upload your deckOne file or many at once.
  2. Choose the effectFade, wipe, fly in and more; one bullet per click or whole boxes.
  3. DownloadExisting animations are kept unless you choose to replace them.

Questions

Will it mess up slides I already animated?

No. Slides that already have animations are left alone unless you switch on “Replace”.

Can I do 30 decks at once?

Yes. Drop them all in; you get one zip back.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs in your browser and your file stays on your device.

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no watermark on your output.
