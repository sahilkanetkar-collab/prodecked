How it stays private

01

The tool comes to your file

When you open a tool, the page loads a small program into your browser. That's the only download.

02

Your deck stays on your device

The program opens the .pptx, makes the change and hands the new file straight back. No server ever sees your slides.

03

Nothing to delete later

Because nothing is uploaded, there's nothing stored. Close the tab and it's gone. Read the privacy page.

Questions

Is ProDecked really free?

Yes. Every tool on this page is free with no sign-up. A paid plan for heavy use (batch jobs, saved brand kits) will come later; the free tools will stay free.

Do you upload my files?

No. The tools run inside your browser, on your own device. Your deck is never sent to a server, which is also why big files are quick.

Will the result open in Google Slides or Keynote?

Yes, the output is a standard .pptx. A few effects (Morph, the countdown bar) need PowerPoint to play.

Does it work on a phone?

Most tools do. For large decks a laptop is faster.

What about old .ppt files?

Open them in PowerPoint or Google Slides and save as .pptx first.
