Add a countdown timer to question slides

A bar runs across the bottom of each question slide and empties over the time you choose. It works in PowerPoint with no add-ins or macros.

How it works

  1. Upload your deckQuestion slides are found automatically.
  2. Set the timeFrom 10 seconds to 5 minutes, starting on click or on its own.
  3. PresentPress F5; the bar runs down on each question.

Questions

Does it need a macro or add-in?

No. The timer is a normal PowerPoint animation, so it runs anywhere the deck opens in PowerPoint.

Can I put a timer on non-question slides?

Not in this version. The timer attaches to slides with A/B/C/D options.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs in your browser and your file stays on your device.

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no watermark on your output.
